1. Acceptance

By accessing and using Tri-Aid, health institutions and their authorized personnel accept these terms and conditions, which reflect the ethical and professional principles of the ACM/IEEE Software Engineering Code of Ethics.

2. Purpose of the service

Tri-Aid is a decision-support tool for triage processes. It does not replace clinical judgment: priority classification is assisted and must always be validated and confirmed by qualified health personnel.

3. Responsibilities

Each health institution is responsible for the accuracy of the data captured, the configuration of habitual ranges, and the clinical use of the information. SoliDevs provides the platform under availability and support agreements.

4. Limitations

Tri-Aid supports the triage process only. It does not replace hospital clinical management, medical diagnosis, or the official emergency channels. Measurements depend on the connected instruments and their maintenance.

SoliDevs may update these terms as the product evolves. Institutions will be notified of material changes before they take effect.
