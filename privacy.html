Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

  1. 01

    Data we collect

    Tri-Aid processes only the information required for triage care: identification data, arrival context, vital sign readings and referral records. No data unrelated to the emergency care process is collected.

  2. 02

    Purpose of the data

    Patient data is used exclusively to register the arrival, capture and evaluate vital signs, classify priority and route the patient to the right specialty. It is never used for advertising or profiling purposes.

  3. 03

    Access and control

    Access to patient information is restricted to authorized health personnel of the institution. Patients may request access, correction or deletion of their personal data through the institution that registered them.

  4. 04

    Data retention

    Clinical records are kept only for the period required by the health institution and applicable regulations. Demo or evaluation data may be purged without prior notice.

  5. 05

    Changes

    SoliDevs may update this policy as the product evolves. Institutions will be notified of material changes before they take effect.
