About

The bridge between measurement instruments and emergency care

In most emergency rooms, vital signs are measured with standalone devices and written down by hand, then typed into a system. That double entry wastes critical minutes and causes errors that can change a patient's priority.

Tri-Aid connects the whole chain: automatic vital-sign capture, assisted priority classification validated by triage staff, and routing to the right specialty.

The problem

In most emergency rooms, vital signs are measured with standalone devices and written down by hand, then typed into a system. That double entry wastes critical minutes and causes errors that can change a patient's priority.

Every minute spent retyping vital signs is a minute the patient does not have.

Features

Everything the triage room needs

  1. 01

    Patient registration

    Digital admission in seconds: identity and contact data recorded once and shared across the emergency visit.

  2. 02

    Automatic vital signs

    Readings flow straight from the measurement instruments into the platform — no manual transcription, no transcription errors.

  3. 03

    Priority classification

    An assisted classification based on habitual ranges suggests the priority level; triage staff validate and confirm it.

  4. 04

    Out-of-range alerts

    Instant notifications when any value exceeds the habitual range, so critical patients escalate without delay.

  5. 05

    Specialty assignment

    Data-driven routing sends every patient to the corresponding medical specialty, reducing wrong referrals and rework.
