About Tri-Aid

The bridge between measurement instruments and emergency care

Why Tri-Aid exists

In most emergency rooms, vital signs are measured with standalone devices and written down by hand, then typed into a system. That double entry wastes critical minutes and causes errors that can change a patient's priority.

Tri-Aid connects the whole chain: from the moment a patient arrives, to the automatic capture of their vital signs, to an assisted priority classification that triage staff always validate.

How it works

From the door to the right specialty

  1. Register

    The patient arrives and is digitally registered by triage staff in seconds.

  2. Measure

    Vital signs are captured automatically from the connected instruments.

  3. Classify

    The platform suggests a priority level; the triage nurse validates it.

  4. Route

    The patient is assigned to the right specialty and staff monitor out-of-range alerts.

For patients

Know your status while you wait

Tri-Aid gives patients visibility of their triage state and the specialty they are being routed to — no more repeating your data at every desk, no more waiting without answers.
